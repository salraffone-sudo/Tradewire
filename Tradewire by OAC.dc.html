About this dashboard. Tradewire tracks how hyperscaler data-center demand is reshaping Big Tech investment in union-backed skilled-trade workforce pipelines. Every dollar amount, training center, state, and worker count is sourced — values not publicly disclosed are shown as "not disclosed" rather than estimated. Funding totals reflect point-confirmed committed and pledged amounts; the true aggregate pledge total is higher where companies disclose only a program description.

Program-level definitions: Apprenticeship = registered/new apprentice slots; Journey-level = upskilling existing journeymen; Pre-apprenticeship = recruitment and awareness pipelines; Both = program spans apprentice recruitment and journey-level upskilling.
